CRITICAL DEFECT CORRECTED
-------------------------
In v8.7.1, the translation engine could treat negative numeric-gap diagnostic
reasons such as `no_numeric_context` as cache-blocking reasons even when no
numeric gap was detected. This prevented ordinary dialogue from being stored
in cache and materially contributed to all-MISS behaviour. v8.7.2 blocks cache
only when a true numeric uncertainty/gap is present.
