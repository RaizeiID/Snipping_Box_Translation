Base: v8.4.4

Summary:
- Adds Number-Safe OCR Guard for GFL2/Lite IDN/Fast IDN numeric dialog issues.
- Preserves useful numbers/measurements before and after translation.
- Rejects UI numeric garbage such as 11+1115141, 30,L, TIIII, LLI-like fragments before cache/translation/learning where possible.
- Stops older OCR digit cleanup from blindly deleting meaningful standalone numbers.
- Keeps v8.4.4 Lite/Lite IDN OCR ladder and Fast retune unchanged.
- Marks this as the final v8.4 polish handoff before v8.5 Lite/Lite IDN optimization.

Recommended final v8.4 test:
- Lite IDN V2, Auto, Hybrid, OCR 45% for heavy-game efficient default.
- Lite IDN V3, Auto, Hybrid, OCR 50% when numeric/dialog accuracy is more important.
- Fast IDN, Auto, Hybrid, OCR 50% when responsiveness is more important than Lite resource saving.

Known limitations:
- Number guard can preserve/correct numbers that OCR partially reads, but cannot recover numbers completely missing from OCR frames.
- True numeric dual-pass OCR and per-region digit OCR are recommended for v8.5.
